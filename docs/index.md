# Welcome to Crystal EMR

**Crystal EMR** is a powerful, easy-to-use clinic management system designed to streamline patient workflows, queue management, and EMR integration.

## Why Choose Crystal?
✅ Practical, Logical: All features, GUI and work-flow of Crystal has been refined by inputs from users over past 20 years. Making it easy, practical and logical to quickly implement it at your hospital.   
✅ Comprehensive Scalable: Crystal provides all modules to meet comprehensive EMR requirements from a small solo-practice clinics to large multi-specialty, multi-branch institutions.  
✅ Option of Secure Local server as well as cloud-based solution.
